How HuntYourTrack Works

A 3-step continuous cycle bridging academic coursework and professional software engineering.

1

Select Track & Take AI Assessment

Declare your specialization in Frontend, Backend, AI, Security, or DevOps. Our diagnostic engine evaluates your current prerequisite skills.

2

Execute Interactive Roadmaps

Work through structured milestones, commit project deliverables to GitHub, and track your graduation velocity in real time.

3

Get Matched with Top Employers

Faculty-verified student profiles are automatically showcased to accredited corporate recruiters for fast-tracked internships.
